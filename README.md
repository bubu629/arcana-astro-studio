## ✨ Core Features

* **🃏 Complete 78-Card Rider-Waite Tarot (100% Offline)**
  * Includes all **22 Major Arcana** and **56 Minor Arcana** (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles) with individualized upright & reversed divinatory meanings based on A.E. Waite's public-domain *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1910)[cite: 8].
  * Supports **1-Card Daily Oracle**, **3-Card Spread** (Past / Present / Future), **5-Card Cross**, and the **10-Card Classic Celtic Cross**[cite: 8].
  * Automatically analyzes **Elemental Balance** (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), Major Arcana weight, and dedicated Love & Career lenses[cite: 8].

* **✨ Real Astronomical Natal Ephemeris & Horoscope**
  * Powered offline by the MIT-licensed **[astronomy-engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy)** (VSOP87 / ELP2000-82B ephemeris) bundled directly inside the APK[cite: 8].
  * Calculates exact ecliptic zodiac signs and degrees (`0.1°` precision) for **Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn**, plus Rising Sign estimation and **Life Path Numerology** (including Master Numbers `11`, `22`, `33`)[cite: 8].
  * Live sky banner displaying real-time planetary placements and exact lunar phase illumination percentage[cite: 8].

* **💞 Planetary & Zodiac Synastry (Compatibility)**
  * Evaluates romantic, business partnership, and friendship compatibility across **Sun, Moon, Venus placements, Elements, and Modalities**, complete with a resonance score and actionable relationship guidance[cite: 8].

* **🤖 (Optional) AI Reader Deep Synthesis**
  * Zero API key required for all offline Tarot spreads, ephemeris charts, and synastry reports[cite: 8].
  * Optionally bring your own Google Gemini API Key to unlock multi-card narrative synthesis and transit readings with built-in multi-model fallback[cite: 8].
